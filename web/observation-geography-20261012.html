How to read these connections

A conditional graph connection has a saved witness when all supplied uncertain road groups are closed. Undetermined has a route with those groups open, but no route to the same fixed destination when they are all closed. Neither result establishes physical road access.

No mapped association means the point or destination lacks an eligible road-node association. Baseline disconnected means the historical graph already lacked a route. It is not a new flood-caused disconnection.

The expanded scenario makes candidate-intersecting, unknown-within-AOI and outside-AOI physical road groups uncertain. All open/closed combinations are permitted; there are no certain observed closures or failure probabilities. Assessed nonwater still does not prove passability.

Displayed roads are only the selected saved witness. Every drawn segment uses its recorded vertices; no line joins a settlement/facility point to a road node. Witnesses use hop-count search, not optimized travel time. Baseline road distance and projected centerline support use different length definitions. Repeated traversals count repeatedly.

Road colors summarize whole segments. Mixed support has more than one positive observation category; the exact within-segment spatial partitions are not supplied. Empty graph routes have null support fractions, never 100% assessed coverage.
