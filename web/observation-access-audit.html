Scenario definitions and limits

Earlier results treat only model new-water candidate segments as uncertain. Expanded results also treat any physical road segment with unknown satellite state or a portion outside the satellite AOI as uncertain. All such groups are open in the optimistic extreme and closed in the pessimistic extreme. All other baseline OSM edges are assumed available. The destination selected in the baseline stays fixed.

Connected in both extremes means the supplied graph still has a witness under all these closures. Undetermined means connectivity depends on the uncertain groups. Neither result establishes real-world safety. Unknown flood-screen support is not evidence that a road is damaged. Old paths are retained as evidence; shared road traversals are counted repeatedly in each route's length summary.

A settlement and its target can snap to the same graph node. Such an empty witness has no road length to assess; its support fractions are unavailable, not 100%. Off-road connections, entrances and facility operation remain unverified.
